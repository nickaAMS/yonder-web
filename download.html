Follow the steps below to install the Namesake preview.

For iPhone and iPad

Apple

Apple shares preview apps through TestFlight, Apple’s official app for trying new apps before they launch on the App Store.

  1. Install Apple TestFlight from the App Store before opening the invitation.
  2. Open the TestFlight invitation and tap Install.
1. Install Apple TestFlight → 2. Open TestFlight invitation →

You may be asked to sign in with your Apple ID.

For Android

Android

Download the latest Namesake preview directly to the Android device you will use.

  1. Download Namesake using the button below.
  2. Allow the download when Android asks for permission.
  3. Open the downloaded file and choose Install.
Download Android app ↓

Finding the latest Android preview…

Google shares preview apps through the Google Play Store. Join the preview group first to unlock the download.

  1. Join the Namesake preview group below and tap Join group, using the email address you use for the Google Play Store.
  2. Open Google Play in Step 2, tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play.
1. Join preview group → 2. Accept invite & install →

Use the same Google account you are signed into on the Play Store.
