Delete your Namesake account

Last updated: October 2026

You can delete your Namesake account and everything saved with it at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, and what is not.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the Namesake app and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account if you are signed in, or Delete all data if you are using Namesake as a guest.
  3. Read the warning and confirm. You will be asked to confirm a second time before anything is erased.

Deletion starts straight away and removes your data from our servers and from your device. You will be signed out when it finishes.

If you cannot open the app

Email hello@trynamesake.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Namesake account". We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your stories, including their text and audio.
  • Story covers.
  • Child and character profiles (family members, pets, toys and villains) and their avatars and character expression sheets.
  • Voice clones, including the recording you gave us and the cloned voice at our voice provider.
  • Connections to Alexa and Toniebox, if you set them up.
  • Public share links to your stories, and the public copy of each shared story.
  • Your settings, consents, welcome gifts, and the usage and generation records we keep for your account.
  • Finally, the sign-in account itself.

What is not deleted

  • Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. On Android, open the Play Store, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Namesake there. On iPhone or iPad, cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
  • Store purchase records. Google Play and Apple keep their own records of purchases. We cannot delete those.
  • Anonymous analytics. We use PostHog (EU servers) to count which screens of the app are used. These events are tied to a random device identifier, not to your account, so we cannot match them to you or remove them individually.

Please note

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We cannot restore an account, stories or voice clones once they are deleted.
