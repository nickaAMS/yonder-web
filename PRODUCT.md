# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents of children aged three to eight who are choosing a calm, trustworthy audio-story ritual for bedtime. They are often tired, short on time, and want an imaginative story that feels personal without giving their child another stimulating screen experience.

## Product Purpose

Yonder creates personalised bedtime stories in which the child and their companions are central. A parent creates and approves each story, then listening continues with the phone screen off or through Bluetooth speakers, Alexa, Google Home, and TonieBox (currently in testing). The launch site should make the App Store launch feel desirable and credible, not collect a waitlist or imply an unavailable download.

## Brand Personality

Illustrated, candlelit, and reassuring. Yonder feels like an heirloom storybook brought quietly into a modern bedtime routine: intimate rather than toy-shop bright, magical rather than sugary, and thoughtfully adult without becoming austere.

## Anti-references

- Bright primary-colour toy aisles, mascots, rewards, streaks, gamification, and addictive-media patterns.
- Photorealistic family lifestyle photography or glossy 3D children’s imagery.
- Generic SaaS layouts, repeated feature-card grids, fake awards, fabricated testimonials, and made-up app UI.
- Misleading claims such as “screen-free”. The honest promise is that the story continues when the phone screen is off.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the child as the hero, proven through a beautiful personalised cover.
2. Make the bedtime ritual tangible: create, approve, play, put the screen away.
3. Use real Yonder art and app states. Every visual proof point must be recognisable as the product.
4. Build parental trust in quietly: no ads, no streaks, no pressure to keep watching.
5. Let watercolour texture, ink detail, warm light, and considered pacing carry the emotion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, high legibility in low light, and descriptive alt text. Do not autoplay audio. Show diverse families through illustrated assets while retaining a consistent watercolour-and-ink style.
