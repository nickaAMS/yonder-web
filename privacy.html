Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Yonder Stories ("Yonder", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families who use our service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Yonder Stories Alexa skill or mobile app, we may collect:

  • Child profile details you provide (e.g. name, age, favourite characters) — used solely to personalise stories.
  • Account information (e.g. email address) when you create a Yonder account.
  • When you request preview access, your email address, chosen device, referral answer, IP address, and an approximate city and country inferred from that IP. We use this to manage the preview programme and prevent abuse.
  • Voice interactions with the Alexa skill, processed by Amazon's Alexa platform in accordance with Amazon's privacy policy.
  • Usage data (e.g. which stories were played) to improve the service.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

2. Children's Privacy

Yonder Stories is designed for use by parents and caregivers on behalf of children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Child profile data is entered by a parent or guardian and is used only to generate personalised story content.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child without parental consent, please contact us at hello@yonderstories.app and we will delete it promptly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver personalised bedtime stories.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Yonder service.
  • To send you service-related communications (e.g. account updates).
  • To contact you about the Yonder preview programme when you ask to join it.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. Story audio files are stored in Supabase cloud storage. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, though no system is completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Yonder Stories uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Amazon Alexa — voice interaction platform
  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Supabase — audio file storage
  • AI providers — for story and audio generation (data is not used for third-party model training)

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, contact us at hello@yonderstories.app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email us at hello@yonderstories.app.
