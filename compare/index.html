Namesake and other
personalised story apps

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

Namesake makes a new story from your child's idea and narrates it once you've checked it. You can play your chosen stories on Alexa or send them to a Yoto card through Namesake. Other apps offer everything from authored books with their name added to personalised videos.

Four alternatives to Namesake

Oscar and SmartDreams make new stories with AI. Little Stories has books written by authors, with your child's name added. StoryMe uses photos to put them inside a digital picture book.

How much of the story changes?

Little Stories keeps the author's plot and adds your child's name. Namesake and Oscar can write a new story around them, including adventures in classic worlds such as The Jungle Book. If pictures matter most, StoryMe makes digital picture books from photos. Namesake can also turn photos into illustrated characters for stories and covers.

Try a story in the language you use at home

Namesake makes stories and narration in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch. Other apps have different language choices, and support for written stories doesn't always include audio. Little Stories, for instance, lists 14 languages overall and professional narration in three. Listen to a sample in the language you'll actually use.

Alexa, Yoto and the speakers at home

Namesake has Alexa setup and story queues, plus a Yoto account connection for sending and managing stories. You can leave the phone with you while the stories play on the device. Google Cast works from Android; Toniebox support is currently experimental.

Questions parents ask

What is an alternative to Oscar Stories for personalised bedtime audio?

Namesake and SmartDreams both make personalised stories with narration. Little Stories is another option if you want books written by authors, with your child's name added. It has professional narration in English, Spanish and Russian, and you can record your own reading.

Is personalised storytelling always AI-generated?

No. The writers and illustrators behind Little Stories make its books without generative AI. The app adds your child's name and gender to those books. Namesake generates a new story from your idea.
