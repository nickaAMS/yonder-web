Personalised stories.
Different ways to tell them.

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

The right personalised storytelling app depends on what you want to make: a new audio adventure, an authored book with your child's name, or a photo-personalised digital keepsake. Namesake focuses on original stories that a parent reviews and approves before narrated bedtime listening. Here are four alternatives worth comparing.

Which story app fits your family?

We publish Namesake, so this is a manufacturer's guide, not an independent ranking. We selected personalised storytelling apps with an App Store presence and substantial US rating histories. New story, personalised book, or both?

Oscar and SmartDreams advertise generated stories and audio. Little Stories personalises a human-authored catalogue. StoryMe centres on photo-personalised books. Namesake creates a new story from a parent-selected idea and familiar details, then asks the parent to approve it. Personalisation is not the same feature across all five apps.

Listen to the actual result

Try the same age and simple story idea in the apps you are considering. Listen for clear narration, the right personal details, and an ending that resolves. A feature list cannot establish story quality, and we have not run a controlled test of the competing apps. Namesake stories are available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch; check text and audio coverage separately in every app.

Questions parents ask

What is an alternative to Oscar Stories for personalised bedtime audio?

Namesake and SmartDreams are options for generated personalised stories with narration. Little Stories offers an authored catalogue with name personalisation and selected-language narration. The best fit depends on the kind of personalisation and parent involvement you want.

Is personalised storytelling always AI-generated?

No. Little Stories by Diveo Media explicitly uses authored and illustrated books without generative AI, personalised with the child's name and gender. Namesake generates a new story from the parent's chosen ingredients.
