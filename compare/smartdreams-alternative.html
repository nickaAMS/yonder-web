Namesake vs SmartDreams

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

SmartDreams and Namesake both make personalised stories with narration. SmartDreams also lists offline access and story sharing. It describes an interface children can use to make stories themselves. In Namesake, you can choose the idea together, then the parent reads and approves the story before narration.

We make Namesake. Details about the other app come from the sources linked below. We haven't tested its paid features.

Making the story together

SmartDreams describes an app children can navigate themselves. If your child enjoys choosing the details and creating a story on screen, that's worth trying. In Namesake the parent handles the review: read the summary or full text, edit anything that doesn't work, and approve the story for narration.

Listening without internet

Both apps support saved stories, and SmartDreams lists offline access. For Namesake, download the audio before you travel or lose your connection. Making a new Namesake story needs internet. We haven't tested SmartDreams' download process, so check it with a saved story before a journey.

Try the current version first

The US App Store lists SmartDreams as a free download. Its latest listed update is December 2023. That date alone doesn't tell us whether the service still works; we haven't installed it to find out. Try creating and playing a story before planning to use it every evening.

Questions parents ask

Does SmartDreams have narration and offline stories?

Its App Store description lists both. We haven't tested them in the app.

Can Namesake play stories offline?

Yes, once the audio has been downloaded. You need an internet connection to make a new story.
