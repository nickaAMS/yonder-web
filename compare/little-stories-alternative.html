Namesake vs Little Stories: generated adventures or authored books?

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

Little Stories by Diveo Media is the stronger fit if you want professionally authored and illustrated books personalised with your child's name, without generative AI. Namesake is an alternative if you want a new AI-generated story shaped around your child's interests, companions and tonight's idea, with parent approval before narrated listening. Both offer audio; they personalise stories in different ways.

We make Namesake. This is our own comparison, based on public product information rather than a paid, hands-on test of the competing app. Unverified features are labelled.

Choose Little Stories for an authored book collection

Little Stories is an established personalised reading app from Diveo Media. The stories follow an editorial process involving authors, illustrators, a composer and narrators. Choose it when you prefer a known, crafted book with your child's name woven in, especially if avoiding generative AI is part of your decision. A fixed catalogue can make repeated favourites easy to return to.

Choose Namesake for a story about tonight's idea

Namesake offers a different kind of personalisation: the story itself is newly generated. A child's favourite toy, a sibling and an idea about a moonlit adventure can become ingredients in the same tale. A parent reviews the result before it joins the ordinary shelf and is narrated. That flexibility comes with a responsibility to check AI-created text for the family who will hear it.

Recording yourself and generating a voice are different

Little Stories lets you record your own reading, which preserves the actual performance you give. That should not be confused with AI voice cloning. Namesake's ordinary narration is generated, and its optional recorded-voice feature is still being developed. If keeping your exact reading is the main goal, try Little Stories' recording workflow first.

Check the language you will actually listen in

A long supported-language list does not necessarily mean every book has professional audio in every language. Little Stories distinguishes its overall language support from its narrated languages. Namesake creates stories and narration in six supported story languages. Test a sample in your preferred language, rather than choosing from the number alone.

Questions parents ask

Does Little Stories use AI to write its books?

Diveo Media's current App Store listing explicitly says its authors and illustrators do not use generative AI. Personalisation inserts your child's details into its authored catalogue.

Can both apps play audio stories?

Yes. Little Stories offers professional narration in selected languages and lets parents record readings. Namesake generates narration for parent-approved stories.
