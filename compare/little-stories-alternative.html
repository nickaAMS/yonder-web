Namesake vs Little Stories

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

Little Stories has books written and illustrated by people, personalised with your child's name. Namesake generates a new story from the details you give it. Little Stories is a good place to start if you want books made without generative AI. Namesake can help when your child wants a story about something very specific. Namesake also connects to Alexa and Yoto, so the stories can play on the devices you already use at bedtime.

We make Namesake. Details about the other app come from the sources linked below. We haven't tested its paid features.

Playing stories away from the phone

You can choose tonight's stories on your phone, then listen on an Echo or send them to your child's Yoto card. Namesake handles the Alexa pairing and story queue, and lets you upload and reorder Yoto stories in the app. Google Cast works from Android. Toniebox support uses Creative-Tonies and is currently experimental.

A book you know, with their name in it

Little Stories' catalogue is made by authors, illustrators, a composer and narrators. The plot is already written; you add your child's name and gender. The developer recommends the books for ages three to six and includes four free books, so you can read together before paying for more.

When they ask for something you can't find in a book

Namesake starts with your child's profile and the idea you give it. Their teddy can go on an adventure with their sister, in a place they've chosen. Once it's written, you can change a detail or a sentence before approving the story for narration. Favourite stories stay on the shelf for another night.

You can record your own reading in Little Stories

Little Stories lets you record yourself reading a book, so your child hears your actual performance. Namesake normally generates the narration; its optional recorded-voice feature is still being developed. Little Stories also has professional narration in English, Spanish and Russian. If you read in one of its other languages, check the audio options before buying.

Buying more books

Little Stories lists both subscriptions and individual book purchases in the US App Store. Namesake's plans set a monthly allowance for new stories. When comparing costs, check how many new stories you expect to make or buy, as well as which audio options the price includes.

Questions parents ask

Does Little Stories use AI?

Diveo Media says its authors and illustrators do not use generative AI. It personalises its existing books with the child's name and gender.

Can both apps read a story aloud?

Yes. Namesake generates narration for approved stories. Little Stories has professional narration in English, Spanish and Russian, and lets you record yourself reading.

Can I play Namesake stories on Alexa, Yoto, Google Cast or Toniebox?

Yes. Namesake has Alexa setup and story queues, plus a Yoto connection for uploading and managing stories. You link the Yoto playlist to a Make Your Own card in the Yoto app. Google Cast is available on Android only. Toniebox support uses Creative-Tonies and is currently experimental.
