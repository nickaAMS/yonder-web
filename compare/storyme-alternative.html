Namesake vs StoryMe

By Namesake · Reviewed 26 October 2026

StoryMe makes a digital picture book from your child's name and photos. Namesake can use photos to make illustrated versions of your child and their companions too, but the result is a narrated story with a cover. StoryMe is worth considering for a book to read and share. Try Namesake if you're mainly looking for bedtime audio.

We make Namesake. Details about the other app come from the sources linked below. We haven't tested its paid features.

A personalised picture book

StoryMe asks you to upload photos and choose a book title. You get a digital book to read and share with family. The listing describes it as suitable for children from birth to eight. We're referring to Personalized Books - StoryMe by Pinata Farms; other services use the same name.

A cover they recognise, and a story they can hear again

Namesake also lets you use photos to create illustrated characters. Your child and their favourite toy can look familiar on the cover as well as appear in the story. You can read the text together or play the narration with the screen off. Accepted stories stay on the child's shelf, each with its own cover.

Check what the purchase includes

StoryMe lists subscriptions as well as separate digital-book purchases. Check whether you're buying one book or signing up for recurring payments. Its description still says hardcover books are coming soon, so we haven't counted printing as available. We also couldn't confirm a narration feature from the listing. The latest listed version is June 2024; we haven't tested the current ordering process.

The listing says hardcover books are coming soon. We haven't confirmed whether they can currently be ordered.
